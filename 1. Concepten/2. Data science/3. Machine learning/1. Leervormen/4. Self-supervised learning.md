- Creates labels or targets from the data itself.
- Masks part of a training instance (input) and uses the unmasked training instance as a label.
	- E.g. masking part of an image or sentence and predicting the missing part.
- Can be used in **transfer learning**.
	- E.g., learns to 'repair' images of different animals. Thereby learning what a species looks like. Then this model is provided with pictures of different specimen and their labels. Thereby learning to classify (name) the species depicted on an image.
