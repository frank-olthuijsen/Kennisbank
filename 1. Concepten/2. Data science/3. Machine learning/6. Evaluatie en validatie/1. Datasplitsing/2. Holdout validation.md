
- Hold out part of the training set, thereby creating a reduced training set and  a **validation set** (or **development set** or **dev set**).
- Train multiple models with various hyperparameters on the reduced training set.
- Select the model that performs best on the validation set.
- Then train the model on the full training set (reduced training + validation).
- Evaluate the model on the test set to get an estimate of the generalization error.
